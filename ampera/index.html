AXIS 4 — SCALABILITY MODEL
Network doubles by end 2027 → contacts grow from 10K to ~20K/month. Budget must stay flat at €1.25M. The only path: reduce cost per contact faster than volume grows.
LEVER 1 · AI DEFLECTION
Absorb volume without adding agents
Every 10pt gain in deflection = ~1,000 contacts/month handled at €0.03–0.06 instead of €4–7. The unit economics are transformative at scale.
At 30% deflection: saves €60–180 vs €4,000–7,000 per 1K contacts
LEVER 2 · BPO RESTRUCTURING
Right-scope the contract, right-price the SLA
BPO cost/contact: €7.2 today. FCR improvement + scope tightening target €6.0 or below. At 6K contacts/month that's €7,200 saved every month.
Every €1 saved per BPO contact = €72K/year at current volume
LEVER 3 · CONTACT RATE REDUCTION
Fix upstream — reduce demand before it arrives
31% of contacts are session failures. Each reliability improvement on the Product side removes contacts before they're ever created. This is the long-term structural play.
Contact rate today: ~8.3%. Industry best-in-class: 3–4%.
